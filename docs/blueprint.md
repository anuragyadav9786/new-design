# **App Name**: ThinkFin Landing Page

## Core Features:

- Header Section: Display the header section with company logo and navigation items.
- Hero Section: Showcase a hero section with a compelling title, description, and call-to-action button.
- Services Section: Present key financial services with icons and descriptions.
- Testimonials Section: Display customer testimonials with a slider component.
- Team Section: Show team member profiles with names, roles, and photos.
- Contact Form: Implement a contact form in the footer to enable leads to submit messages.
- Footer Contact Info: Display contact information such as address, phone number, and email in the footer section.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft blue (#5DADE2) to convey trust and financial security.
- Background color: Light grey (#F5F7FA) for a clean, modern feel.
- Accent color: Complementary teal (#2A9D8F) for call-to-actions and highlights.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif for a modern and accessible reading experience.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk', a sans-serif, for titles and headings.
- Use modern, flat icons related to finance and investment services.
- Design a clean and modern layout with clear sections and ample white space.
